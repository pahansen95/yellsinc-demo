Our Impact

Since 2008, YELLS has been transforming lives in the Franklin Gateway community of Marietta, Georgia. Founded by Marietta High School teacher Laura Keefe, our organization has grown from a small mentoring initiative to a comprehensive youth development program.

We envision communities where youth have voice and value and ensure they possess the leadership skills, confidence, character, and collaborative networks needed to shape their world.
