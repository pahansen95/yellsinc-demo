Frequently Asked Questions

How can I enroll my child in YELLS programs?

Contact us using the form above or email us at info@yellsinc.org. We'll provide you with enrollment information and let you know about current program availability.

What ages do you serve?

YELLS programs serve youth from elementary school through young adulthood. Our Afterschool Program serves elementary and middle school students, while our Mentoring Program and Community Action Cafe serve middle and high school students.

How can I volunteer?

We're always looking for dedicated volunteers! Visit our Volunteer page to learn about opportunities, or contact us directly to discuss how you can help.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes! YELLS, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent of the law. You will receive a receipt for your records.

Where is YELLS located?

YELLS is based in the Franklin Gateway community of Marietta, Georgia, serving youth in the greater Marietta area.

About YELLS

YELLS, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization empowering youth in the Franklin Gateway community of Marietta, Georgia since 2008.
